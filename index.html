Frequently asked questions.

Are your prices final?

Prices are fixed per package. After a free consultation we share a written quotation based on your features, integrations and timeline. Hosting, domains, paid plugins, app-store fees and AI usage fees are billed at actuals and always estimated upfront.

How long does a project take?

A landing page takes about 5 to 7 days and a business website 3 to 4 weeks. Mobile apps take 6 to 14 weeks and custom AI solutions 2 to 16 weeks, depending on the plan.

Do we own the code and data?

Yes. Source code, designs and data belong to you after final payment.

What support do you provide after launch?

Every package includes 30 days of free post-launch fixes. Monthly Care Plans (Basic, Growth and Premium) cover monitoring, security updates, backups and changes.

Can you work with clients outside Hyderabad?

Yes. We operate as a virtual team and work with clients anywhere through video calls, shared boards and weekly demos.

Is my data safe with AI solutions?

We design AI assistants with private data handling, role-based access and security reviews, and we explain clearly which third-party AI services are used.
